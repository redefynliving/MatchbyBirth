How Match by Birth Calculates Astrological Compatibility

Match by Birth is an astrological compatibility calculator that compares two people or groups using birth date placements. Astrological Elements and Chart Logic

Every birth date corresponds to unique planetary placements. We map these placements to the four classical elements to understand your core energetic makeup:

  • Fire Signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius): Passionate, active, expressive, and highly driven.
  • Earth Signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn): Practical, stable, reliable, and grounded in reality.
  • Air Signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius): Intellectual, communicative, curious, and social.
  • Water Signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces): Sensitive, emotional, intuitive, and deeply empathetic.

By analyzing elements and aspect degrees, we determine if your charts form harmonious flows (such as trines or sextiles) or challenging lessons (such as squares or oppositions).
