# Match by Birth Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 1: Make The Paid Report Worth Buying

Timeline: 1-2 weeks  
Goal: the paid report must feel specific enough that AI does not feel like an easier substitute.

Build:

- Paid report sales page
- Sample paid report page
- Report sections:
  - Why this feels familiar
  - What pulls you together
  - Where the rhythm clashes
  - What each person may need
  - What each person may misread
  - Green flags
  - Yellow flags
  - What not to ignore
  - What to ask before getting attached
  - Message you could send
  - Final read: chemistry, comfort, or chaos

Quality bar:

- no generic zodiac filler
- at least 8 specific paragraphs
- one practical message prompt
- one "watch this" warning
- one reassuring but non-deterministic close

## Phase 2: Turn Free Results Into Shareable Objects

Timeline: 1-2 weeks  
Goal: make the result something people send, save, and screenshot.

Build:

- share card design
- share link with opaque result slug
- short public preview page
- "send this to them" CTA
- saved image export

Free result structure:

- Overall read
- Strongest pull
- Main friction
- What they may misread
- Talk about this first
- Unlock the private read

## Phase 3: Tool Landing Pages

Timeline: 2-4 weeks  
Goal: rank pages that directly produce calculator usage.

Build:

- `/tools/birth-date-compatibility`
- `/tools/moon-sign-compatibility`
- `/tools/life-path-compatibility` upgrade
- `/tools/crush-birthday-compatibility` upgrade
- `/tools/friendship-compatibility`

Each page needs:

- embedded tool or direct calculator handoff
- static crawlable content
- FAQ schema
- Article/HowTo schema where appropriate
- internal links to paid report and methodology

## Phase 4: Content Cluster

Timeline: 4-12 weeks  
Goal: publish at a natural cadence while building authority.

Publish:

- 2 posts per week
- 1 tool/support page every other week
- 1 sample/report improvement per month

Quality bar:

- each article has one unique scenario
- each article links to a tool
- no bulk same-day drops
- no repeated structure across every post

## Phase 5: Measurement

Timeline: ongoing  
Goal: know whether the site is getting closer to money.

Track:

- search clicks
- calculator starts
- result completions
- share clicks
- paid report clicks
- checkout starts
- purchases
- email signups
- article-to-calculator conversion

Do not judge by traffic alone. A post that sends 40 people to the calculator is better than a post that gets 1,000 empty visits.
